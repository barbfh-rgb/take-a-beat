Take a Beat with Barb

A pause, whenever you want one. Draw a card and let the phrase settle before you decide what it means for you today.

How to use this

Choose one card for a single thought, three or five for a fuller reading, or Liminal when you are standing between one thing and the next. Press Draw. Read the phrase first and the meaning after.

The line under the date names the phase of the moon and the draw that belongs to it, so you always know how many cards today is asking for. The button carries a ✦ to match. You are welcome to ignore it and choose your own.

Under the ⚙ you will find a seed. Keep it if you ever want to pull the same reading again.

Your reflection

Write underneath any reading and press "Keep this reading" to hold it. Everything you keep appears under "Your readings", newest at the top.

Each one records the phase of the moon you drew under, so once you have a few kept you will find a pattern at the top of that list showing which phases you return to and how often you took the draw the moon asked for.

Your records

Everything you write is held on this device only. Keep a backup so nothing is lost if you change phone, clear your browser or reinstall the app.

Save a backup creates a small file of every reading you have kept. On a phone you will be offered the share sheet, so you can send it to your files, your cloud storage or your own email. On a computer it lands in your downloads folder. Do this every few weeks.

Restore from a backup lets you choose a backup file you saved earlier and your readings come back. Anything already kept is added to rather than replaced.

Print or save as PDF opens everything you have kept ready to print, or choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialogue to keep a readable copy.

Privacy

All your data is saved privately on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere, so do save yourself a backup now and then.
